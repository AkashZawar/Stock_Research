What is this tab for?

Use this to assess a fund scheme before an SIP or a lump sum. A headline one-year number depends heavily on when you happened to start, so this tab leans on rolling returns and risk-adjusted measures to show what a range of entry dates actually produced. Search by scheme name - Direct Growth plans are ranked first.

What it shows you

  • Fund profile - category, expense ratio, AUM, and latest NAV where published
  • Performance and risk - returns to 5Y CAGR, volatility, downside deviation, and max drawdown
  • Rolling 1-year returns - median, quartiles, best and worst windows, and how often a year finished positive
  • Return per unit of risk - Sharpe and Sortino against a stated risk-free rate
  • Versus market reference - excess return over the index grossed up for its dividend yield, plus beta and active risk
  • Annual returns and comparison - calendar-year record against category peers
  • Top holdings, allocation plan, and data checks - portfolio, a suitability read, and which inputs were available
