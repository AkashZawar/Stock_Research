What is this tab for?

Use this for a ready-made shortlist of ideas. It collects stock calls that already carry analyst coverage or institutional (FII/DII) backing and presents them as actionable trades with levels attached. Treat these as leads to verify, not conclusions - run anything that interests you through the Stock Analysis tab before acting.

What it shows you

  • Recommendation list - each idea with its buy level, sell target, and stop loss
  • Expected upside - the move implied from the current price to the target
  • Suggested hold duration - the horizon the call is framed on
  • Heatmap view - the same ideas ranked visually by conviction and upside
  • Intraday 3-5% watchlist - names screened for a same-session move
