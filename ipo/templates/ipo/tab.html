What is this tab for?

Use this to judge an IPO before you apply, and to see how recent ones actually did. It pairs the exchange's own issue and bidding data with the median grey-market premium across five independent trackers, so you can see both what the market is paying and how confident that number is. Grey market data is unofficial and unregulated - treat it as sentiment, not as a price.

What it shows you

  • Listed in the last 7 days - issue price, actual listing price, current price, and whether the listing gain has held
  • Open and upcoming issues - price band, dates, and expected listing price for anything open now or opening within 7 days
  • GMP consensus - the median premium across trackers, plus how far apart they are
  • Live subscription - overall times subscribed, split by QIB, NII, and retail
  • Flag - a green, amber, or red read combining premium and demand
  • Mainboard and SME - both boards, tagged

How to read the flag

The flag scores the grey-market premium (55%), overall subscription (30%), and QIB participation (15%), using only the parts that have data yet, and then pulls the result toward neutral by however much evidence is missing. A premium quoted by one tracker moves it far less than the same premium on four, and a book still taking bids counts for less than a closed one - Indian issues fill on the last day, so an early figure is incomplete rather than weak. Where too little has been confirmed to call it either way, the flag reads Indicative rather than stating a verdict it cannot support.
